# Support

Choose the channel that matches your request.

| Request | Channel | Link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A question about how to use Harnessforce | Discussions, Q&A | [Ask a question](https://github.com/lambda-script/harnessforce-agent/discussions/categories/q-a) |
| A feature request | Discussions, Ideas | [Share an idea](https://github.com/lambda-script/harnessforce-agent/discussions/categories/ideas) |
| A bug you can reproduce | Issue, bug report form | [Report a bug](https://github.com/lambda-script/harnessforce-agent/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) |
| Billing, your account or your Workspace data | Email | [support@lambdascript.dev](mailto:support@lambdascript.dev) |
| An Enterprise contract | The contact form on the pricing page | [Talk to us](https://harnessforce.dev/en/pricing#enterprise) |
| A security vulnerability | Private report | See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) |

If you are not sure that a bug is reproducible, start in Q&A. When we confirm a bug, we open an issue
for it.

All categories are in [Discussions](https://github.com/lambda-script/harnessforce-agent/discussions).

## Discussions and issues are public

Anyone can read discussions and issues. Do not post your Workspace data, ingest keys, API tokens or
prompts (session content). Remove them from logs and output before you post. For anything about your
Workspace data, use email instead.

## Response time

We aim to send a first reply to email, the Enterprise contact form, Q&A, Ideas and issues within 48
hours, counting only our business days (Monday to Friday, excluding Japanese public holidays, in Japan
Standard Time). This is a target, not a service level agreement.

Other Discussions categories, such as General, are for users to talk with each other and are outside
this target. We move questions and feature requests posted there to Q&A or Ideas, and count the 48
hours from the move. Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
