Add the experimental `diagram` skill (`/harnessforce:diagram`), which draws architecture, CI/CD, state, error-flow, ER, Gantt and sequence diagrams of a repository in Mermaid and renders them with product logos. Rendering downloads mermaid-cli and Chromium on first use, and the logos come from public icon sites.
